## Purpose

This is the foundational repository for Altman Family Group and all its ventures, beginning with AltmanAI.

It exists to define, protect, and evolve the core philosophy, principles, and frameworks that guide every decision, product, relationship, and line of code we write.

Everything we build flows from here. This repo is the operating system for how we think, build, and measure success.

## Our North Star

We are building **human-first artificial intelligence**.

Not AI that optimizes for engagement, addiction, extraction, or the replacement of human judgment — but AI that serves human flourishing, amplifies human capability, earns trust through verifiable positive impact, and respects human dignity.

**Humanity Leads. Intelligence Follows.**

We use AI aggressively as a force multiplier. Humans remain the final authority on vision, ethics, direction, and meaning.

## The P-A-I-H-I Framework

**P-A-I-H-I** (Proof of AI-Human Impact) is our standard for designing, building, and evaluating every system.

It requires that major initiatives demonstrate:

- **P**roof — Measurable, documented outcomes in the real world (not promises or vanity metrics)
- **A**ccountability — Clear human ownership, oversight, and responsibility at every layer
- **I**mpact — Verifiable positive effect on actual human lives, communities, and potential
- **H**umanity — Centered on human agency, dignity, truth, and long-term well-being
- **I**ntegration — Thoughtful, respectful partnership between human intelligence and machine intelligence

P-A-I-H-I is not a marketing slogan. It is an internal discipline we apply rigorously and improve over time with real usage data from our products.

## Core Principles

1. **Truth First** — We seek and communicate truth, even when it is uncomfortable or inconvenient.
2. **Human Dignity** — Every system we build must respect and elevate the human being at its center.
3. **Verifiable Impact** — We measure what actually matters and prove it with evidence.
4. **Long-term Orientation** — We build for generations, not quarters or funding cycles.
5. **Radical Usefulness** — Beautiful technology that solves real problems for real people in the real world.
6. **Grounded Ambition** — Pittsburgh-rooted, practical, and relentlessly execution-focused.
7. **Collaborative Evolution** — We build in the open with aligned humans and improve through rigorous feedback.

## How We Build

- Every repository in this organization ultimately serves the principles defined here.
- Contributions and decisions are evaluated first on alignment with these principles.
- We use AI tools powerfully and without apology — but humans set the vision, make the final calls, and carry the responsibility.
- We prioritize clarity over cleverness, impact over hype, and truth over optics.
- We document our reasoning so others can learn, challenge, and improve upon it.
