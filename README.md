## Purpose

This repository is the foundational operating system for Altman Family Group and every venture under it, beginning with AltmanAI.

It defines the non-negotiable principles, frameworks, and decision-making standards that guide all product work, team behavior, partnerships, and long-term direction. Every other repository in this organization ultimately serves what is established here.

This is not a marketing site. It is the living source of truth for how we build.

## North Star

We build **human-first artificial intelligence** — technology that amplifies human capability, protects human agency, and delivers verifiable positive impact on real lives.

We are not optimizing for engagement, extraction, or the automation of human judgment. We are building AI that earns trust through measurable benefit and respects the human at the center of every system.

**Humanity Leads. Intelligence Follows.**

AI is a powerful tool. Humans remain the final authority on vision, ethics, and meaning.

## The P-A-I-H-I Framework

**P-A-I-H-I** (Proof of AI-Human Impact) is our standard for designing, building, and evaluating every system we create.

It requires that major initiatives demonstrate clear evidence across five dimensions:

- **Proof** — Measurable, documented outcomes in the real world rather than promises or vanity metrics.
- **Accountability** — Explicit human ownership and oversight at every meaningful layer.
- **Impact** — Verifiable positive effect on actual human lives, communities, and potential.
- **Humanity** — Centered on human dignity, agency, truth-seeking, and long-term well-being.
- **Integration** — Thoughtful, respectful partnership between human intelligence and machine capability.

P-A-I-H-I is an active discipline, not a slogan. We apply it rigorously, test it against real product work, and improve it over time.

## Core Operating Principles

1. **Truth First**  
   We seek and communicate truth, even when it is inconvenient. Accuracy and intellectual honesty are non-negotiable.

2. **Human Dignity**  
   Every system we build must respect and elevate the human being. We do not optimize for replacement or reduction of human value.

3. **Verifiable Impact**  
   We measure what actually matters and prove outcomes with evidence. Good intentions are not sufficient.

4. **Long-term Orientation**  
   We build for generations, not quarters. Short-term gains that compromise the long-term mission are rejected.

5. **Radical Usefulness**  
   We prioritize technology that solves real problems for real people with clarity and reliability over novelty.

6. **Grounded Ambition**  
   We are Pittsburgh-rooted and execution-focused. Vision without disciplined delivery is noise.

7. **Collaborative Evolution**  
   We build in the open with aligned humans and improve through rigorous, principled feedback.

## How We Build

- Every repository in this organization ultimately serves the principles and framework defined here.
- Contributions and decisions are evaluated first on alignment with these standards.
- We use AI tools aggressively as force multipliers, but humans set direction, make final calls, and carry responsibility.
- We prioritize clarity over cleverness, evidence over hype, and long-term integrity over short-term optics.
- We document our reasoning so others can understand, challenge, and improve upon it.

## Scope & Relationship to Other Repositories

`foundation` is the philosophical and operational core.

Product repositories (DailyPilot, Sophia, EvidenceOS, etc.) contain implementation, features, and domain-specific logic. They are expected to reference and remain consistent with the principles established in this repository.

When in doubt about direction, trade-offs, or priorities, return to this repository first.
