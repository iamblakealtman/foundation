### Reporting Requirements

Please include:

* **A description of the vulnerability.**
* **The affected repository, component, or version.**
* **Steps necessary to reproduce the issue safely.**
* **Potential security or human impact.**
* **Relevant, sanitized supporting evidence.**
* **Recommended remediation, if available.**

**Do not publicly disclose sensitive vulnerability details, credentials, personal information, or exploitable security weaknesses before coordinated review.**

## 5. Responsible Disclosure and Good-Faith Research

Security researchers are expected to:

* Act **lawfully**, **ethically**, and in **good faith**.
* **Minimize potential harm.**
* Respect individual **privacy** and **confidentiality**.
* **Avoid unauthorized access or data collection.**
* **Avoid disruption of systems or services.**
* Stop testing if sensitive information or unintended access is encountered.
* Cooperate with coordinated remediation efforts.

**Good-faith reports will be evaluated on their technical merits and potential impact.**

**This policy does not grant unrestricted testing authorization, immunity from applicable law, or permission to access third-party systems.**

## 6. Human-First Artificial Intelligence Security

AltmanAI engineering principles require that artificial intelligence systems remain accountable to authorized human oversight.

**Security-sensitive AI operations must incorporate, where applicable:**

* **Human authorization and intervention mechanisms.**
* **Least-privilege access controls.**
* **Traceable decision-making.**
* **Protection against prompt injection and unauthorized tool execution.**
* **Data privacy and confidentiality safeguards.**
* **Secure handling of credentials and secrets.**
* **Reversible actions and controlled failure mechanisms.**
* **Evidence-based validation of operational outcomes.**

**Artificial intelligence systems must not independently assume executive, legal, or governance authority.**

## 8. Secure Software Engineering

**The following practices form the intended security baseline for maintained software projects:**

* **Human-reviewed code changes.**
* **Secure authentication and authorization.**
* **Dependency vulnerability monitoring.**
* **Secret detection and protection.**
* **Input validation and secure error handling.**
* **Appropriate automated security testing.**
* **Protected release workflows.**
* **Documented incident response procedures.**
* **Controlled deployment and rollback capabilities.**

**Security controls shall be evaluated according to their effectiveness and verified implementation rather than unsupported assurances.**

---

## 9. Incident Response

**Reported security incidents should be evaluated through the following process:**

1. **Receive and document the report.**
2. **Assess credibility, severity, scope, and human impact.**
3. **Contain affected systems where necessary, under authorized human oversight.**
4. **Investigate the underlying cause.**
5. **Develop and test appropriate remediation.**
6. **Obtain required human authorization.**
7. **Implement the approved remediation.**
8. **Validate outcomes and document lessons learned.**

**Human safety, privacy, and operational integrity shall remain the primary considerations throughout incident response.**

**Response and remediation timelines depend on severity, complexity, and available resources.**
